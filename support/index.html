A quick guide to your sound notebook.

Your first beat

  1. Select a pad in Sample, then open the sound library to preview and load a sound. You can also use Import or Mic.
  2. Choose bank A, B, C or D to access all 64 pads.
  3. Adjust the selected sample’s Start and End points in Sample.
  4. Open Sequence, choose a pattern and a pad, then tap steps to place hits. Press Play in the bottom transport.
  5. Open Perform, choose an effect and adjust its amount. Resample records the processed output into a pad.
  6. Use the track controls to arrange patterns. Choose Export WAV, then With FX or Dry, and use Share WAV to save or send the file.
  7. Tap Save to keep your current session on this device.

Need a tip inside the app?

Enable Help, then tap a highlighted control to read its explanation. Tap Done to return to making music.

Common questions

The microphone is unavailable.

Allow microphone access in your device’s Settings for Sampler Sketch. Check that a microphone is connected, close apps that may be using audio input and try again. If using an audio interface, check its input connection. The sound library and imported samples work without microphone permission.

Where are my saved sessions?

Save keeps a local snapshot of the current samples, trims, patterns, tempo and effect settings. Use the session menu to load your saved session. There is no account or cross-device sync. Removing the app removes its local data; export finished audio before uninstalling.

Can I back up a project using WAV export?

WAV export creates an audio file, not an editable project backup. Keep the app installed to retain its local saved session. A full project backup and transfer feature is not currently included.

When do sequence edits become audible?

Edits made while a pattern is playing are prepared for an upcoming loop boundary. Allow a loop to complete. Track playback uses the arrangement captured when playback starts.

How do I export on iPad?

Choose Export WAV in the track controls and wait for it to finish. Tap Share WAV to open the sharing menu beside the share button, then choose Save to Files or another destination.

What are the recording limits?

Samples and individual recordings are limited to 30 seconds, with up to 180 seconds of source audio across the session. Trim changes playback points without removing the original source from memory.
