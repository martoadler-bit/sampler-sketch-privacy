Privacy policy for the Sampler Sketch iPhone and iPad app, provided by Martin Adler.

Information handled by the app

Sampler Sketch processes microphone recordings, imported audio, sample edits, patterns, tempo, effect settings and saved sessions locally on your device. The app does not transmit this content to the developer or operate a cloud storage service.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Microphone and file access

Microphone access is requested when you choose to record. It is used to create the sample you request. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device’s Settings and continue using built-in or imported sounds.

When you import audio, you choose the file using the system file picker. The app uses that file to create a local sample.

Storage, backups and deletion

Saved sessions and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Sampler Sketch does not provide its own cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in iCloud or device backups under Apple’s policies.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. Offloading an app may retain its documents and data. Exported files, copies shared with others and existing device backups must be managed separately in their respective locations.

Sharing is your choice

Audio is sent to another app or destination only when you choose to export and share it using the system share sheet. The selected service’s privacy practices apply to that copy.

Support communications

If you email martoadler@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and any information or files you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email providers may process those messages to deliver and store them. Support communications are retained only as needed to handle your request and any applicable obligations. You may contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

This website

This support website does not add advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website processing is separate from the app’s on-device audio processing.

Children’s privacy

The app does not request personal information or provide accounts. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact martoadler@gmail.com so the request can be reviewed.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version and revision date will be published on this page.

For privacy questions, contact Martin Adler at martoadler@gmail.com.
